# Changelog

## v1.0.1

- Widen the common cartridge stop to 83.5 mm, leaving 0.25 mm nominal clearance per side.
- Remove rear lid lettering while retaining the sliding guides, both locking pins and cable opening.
- Supply updated STL, STEP and native PLA projects, including the complete eight-part kit on one plate.
- Use one project version in VERSION, with automatic checked print releases on main pushes.

The shell, drive cartridges and both pins from v1.0.0 remain compatible. Geometry and actual slicing are checked; full-kit physical endurance and thermal validation are not claimed.

## v1.0.0

Initial published DIY 3D-printable enclosure for Samsung T7, T7 Shield and T9. Existing published assets remain unchanged.
